About Me

My name is Musenga Hope Sichula, and I am a Data Science student at Mulungushi University in the School of Engineering and Technology. I am currently taking ICT251 Web Technologies, which is teaching me how to structure, style and bring web pages to life using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
